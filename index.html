Our Purpose

The Big Plan

01
Regional Growth

Investing in Lifesaving

Our aim is to put what we earn back into building new lifesaving clubs across Suffolk and supporting existing activities throughout the wider East Region.

  • Helping new club startups in Suffolk
  • Supporting beach and pool initiatives
  • Expanding regional lifesaving coverage
02
Grassroots Impact

Developing Vital Skills

Through volunteer teams, clubs deliver RLSS UK programmes to children aged 8+ in pool and open water settings.

  • Water safety and aquatic rescue skills
  • Pool and open water training sessions
  • Opportunities to compete in lifesaving sport
