Privacy Policy

1. Who We Are

Amble is a club management platform operated by AER+ Training Limited (Registered in England and Wales 16836702) ("Amble", "we", "us" and "our").

We provide software that helps clubs and member-based organisations manage memberships, attendance, communications, payments, qualifications, safeguarding records and related administrative activities.

We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) under registration reference ZC153598.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights in relation to data for which we are the controller, please contact us at: Email: hello@ambleapp.co.uk

2. Controller and Processor Responsibilities

Under UK data protection law, responsibility for personal data depends on whether an organisation acts as a controller or processor.

Clubs are the Data Controller: Each club using Amble is responsible for the personal data it records about its members, parents, guardians, staff, volunteers and emergency contacts.

The club decides what information is collected, why it is collected, how long it should be retained, and who may access it.

Amble is the Data Processor: Amble processes member information solely on the instructions of the club. We do not use club member data for advertising, profiling or marketing purposes.

Amble is the Controller for Certain Data: Amble acts as controller for account administration data, subscription and billing information, support enquiries, service usage information, and operational logs/security records.

3. Information We Collect

Information We Control: Names, email addresses, account credentials, organisation details, billing information, support communications, and technical/security logs. Payment card details are processed by Stripe; we do not store full payment card numbers.

Information Processed on Behalf of Clubs: Member names, dates of birth, addresses, email addresses, telephone numbers, parent/guardian information, emergency contact details, attendance records, qualifications/awards, medical/allergy information, consent records, and safeguarding information.

4. How We Use Information

We use information we control to provide and maintain the service, authenticate users, process subscriptions and payments, provide technical support, respond to enquiries, monitor security, investigate misuse, improve platform performance, and comply with legal obligations.

We process club member information solely for the purpose of providing the Amble service to the relevant club.

5. Lawful Bases for Processing

For information we control, our lawful bases are Contract (to provide access and manage subscriptions), Legitimate Interests (to maintain security, prevent fraud, provide support, and improve reliability), and Legal Obligation.

6. Children's Data

Many organisations using Amble work with children and young people. We expect clubs using Amble to comply with applicable safeguarding and data protection requirements when collecting and managing children's information.

7. Cookies and Analytics

We use a limited number of cookies and similar technologies necessary for user authentication, session management, and security functions.

8. Service Providers and Sub-Processors

9. International Transfers

Where international transfers occur outside the United Kingdom, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including adequacy regulations and standard contractual clauses under UK data protection law.

10. Data Retention

Club data is retained while a club maintains an active account. Following account closure, data may be retained for a limited period to enable export and recovery before deletion. Financial records are retained for up to six years where required by law.

11. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures including encryption in transit, secure authentication systems, role-based access controls, and regular backups to protect personal information.

12. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, individuals may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, or receive their data in a portable format. For club data, requests should be directed to the club as controller.

13. Complaints

If you have concerns, please contact us first at hello@ambleapp.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; the current version will always be published on our website.
